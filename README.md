# Trace — A Storybook Video.js Skin

**Trace** is an educational Video.js v10 skin designed for kids, transforming the video player into an illustrated children’s book and interactive adventure field journal.

Instead of a conventional dark, glassy media bar, Trace turns watching a lesson or story into an expressive, hands-on experience where young viewers can pause any moment, doodle directly onto the scene, collect their favorite frames in a keepsake filmstrip, and export their sketches as a storybook scroll.

## Visual Style

- **Illustrated Children’s Book Frame:** Video sits inside warm stitched parchment panels, oak-ink borders, rounded corners, and soft tactile book shadows.
- **Day & Bedtime Palettes:** Adapts seamlessly between a sunlit cream-parchment daytime journal and a cozy dark-espresso bedtime field journal.
- **Animated Woodland Controls:** Every button and indicator feels hand-drawn and alive:
  - **Sunflower Medallion:** A golden blooming sunflower for play, pause, and restart.
  - **Ladybug Seeker:** An animated ladybug walking along a leafy garden vine, looking around curiously when paused, and fluttering open her spotted wings when hopping between chapters.
  - **Songbird & Acorn Volume:** A cheerful singing songbird perched on a branch with floating notes that curls up to sleep when muted, paired with an acorn slider thumb.
  - **Carved Storybook Chrome:** A wooden ship’s helm for settings, fluffy cloud captions, rustic picture-frame PiP and cast icons, and parchment caption plaques with friendly rounded typography.
- **Interactive Sketchbook Experience:** Built-in pen, marker, textured crayon, highlighter, text, and eraser tools let kids annotate moments right on the video, browse saved moments in a sprocketed filmstrip, jump back to any scene, and download an illustrated storybook PNG scroll.
- **Welcoming & Accessible:** Generous touch targets, high-contrast parchment surfaces readable over bright snow or dark night scenes, full keyboard support, and gentle calm behavior when reduced motion is preferred.
